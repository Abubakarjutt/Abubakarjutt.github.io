About

Most GenAI pilots look great in a demo and break on real data. I work on the part that comes after: getting them into production and keeping them working.

Recently I built a plain-English data and HR assistant for a 3,000-employee conglomerate, using LangGraph, AWS Bedrock and FastAPI with streaming. The client’s own engineers now maintain it. I’ve also shipped churn, segmentation and lead-scoring models for a Gulf economic zone, running in production with drift detection and automated retraining.

My path runs from research and computer vision to generative AI, with an M.Sc. in Data Science along the way. I’m always happy to talk with people building agents or trying to get a pilot over the line.
